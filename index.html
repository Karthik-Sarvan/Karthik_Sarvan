🔌 Maintainer: Mimic Hardware Emulation

Mimic: Hardware-in-the-Loop Test Harness
mimic.aegiondynamic.com
Lead Architect & Maintainer · Bare-Metal C & Rust

Firmware teams traditionally wait weeks to months for physical PCB revisions. Mimic bridges that gap by running directly on bare-metal STM32 microcontrollers, acting as a physical I2C/SPI slave peripheral to emulate complex multi-sensor payloads and synthetic failure modes in real time.

This enables automated integration testing of strict PID control loops, bus recovery handlers, and edge conditions in continuous integration before silicon arrives.
